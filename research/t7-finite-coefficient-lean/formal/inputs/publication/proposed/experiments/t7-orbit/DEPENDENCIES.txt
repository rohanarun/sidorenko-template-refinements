Main theorem:
  RL(T7,D), for one fixed even D>=12, implies a finite rational symmetric
  [0,1] kernel U of positive mean mu with t(H,U)<mu^30, hence a finite
  simple graph G with an edge and t(H,G)<(2e(G)/|V(G)|^2)^30.

  with the pre-coloring hypothesis extraction in Task4 section 2.

  The corollary then has no remaining conjectural analytic hypothesis.

    Required: finite simple sphere triangulation; each edge has two
    distinct incident faces; connected face dual; Hamiltonian polygon
    exposures; rational coverages a_e>=1/3; sum incident a_e=2;
    total excess sum(a_e-1/3)=2; maximum point degree <=6.
    T7 supplies all these in theorem section 3. Degree-three vertices
    and separating triangles are permitted. No minimum degree four,
    no thirteen-point count, and no discrete refinement are imported.

A1. Rank-layer definitions and exact normalization.
    L3 lines 112-132; T4 lines 74-88.
    Symmetric matrices, positive half-rank r=D/2, quotient-form sign,
    positive rational signed-layer probability and mean-one K.
    Reversed differences are handled with c0=chi((-1)^r), unchanged
    four-sign parity. Theorem section 2 states the complete premise.

A2. Elementary symmetric-matrix counts.
    L3 section 3.1, lines 134-168, equations (3.2)-(3.3).

A3. Restriction-sign balance with valid conditioning.
