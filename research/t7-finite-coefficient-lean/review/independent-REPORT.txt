EXACT MATHEMATICAL CONTENT

Let beta=2^564, and set

  Z_I = 2/beta^333 + 2/beta^592 + 2/beta^925 + 1/beta^1332,
  Z_J = 8/beta^3 + 2/beta^5,
  eta = beta^(-8314) / (2^60 * Z_I^7 * Z_J^10).

The bound is an inequality of exact rational numbers, with eta>0 proved.
pointNormalizer_source, faceNormalizer_source and eta_source identify the exact
source constants, so eta is not a freely chosen or assumed positive parameter.

For every point map a:Fin 7 -> Fin 7 and face map b:Fin 10 -> Fin 10, let C(a,b)
be the full all-nonempty coefficient: sum over all assignments of a nonempty
subset of the thirty corners to each of fifteen point edges, of the product of
the thirty actual local pair moments. Let

  Q(a,b) = -sum_i 37*d(a(i))^2 - sum_j ||m(b(j))||^2
           + sum_(i,j in K) [74*d(a(i))+2*m(b(j))(d(a(i)))].

The theorem proves strict negativity for the literal finite expectation

  t0 = averagedCoefficient
     = E_(a from pointPrior^7) E_(b from facePrior^10)
         [thinnedCoefficient(a,b)]
     = (Z_I^7 Z_J^10)^(-1) * beta^(-13380)
         * sum_(all a,b) C(a,b) * beta^(Q(a,b))
     < -eta.

Lean expresses negative exponents through positive reciprocal powers; its
exponentCost_gap proves cost=8314+(5066-Q).toNat after establishing Q<=5066.
Thus Nat subtraction/toNat do not silently truncate an unproved positive score.

their actual stochastic coefficients each equal -4^(-30), giving -2^(-59).

give |pairMoment|<=1, and the full coefficient is bounded in magnitude by 2^450.

7^7*10^10 = 8,235,430,000,000,000 < 2^54. Every lower-score term has relative
weight <=1/beta. The concrete remainder, with the two orbit terms removed, is
proved to satisfy

  -2^(-60) < finiteRemainder < 2^(-60).

The normalized full sum is therefore less than -2^(-60). The actual prior and
thinning weight for EVERY type map is proved to equal its relative score weight

the displayed negative bound. No negativity, coefficient bound, score-realization
or remainder estimate is supplied as a premise to the headline theorem.

   including empty masks, proving first-moment cancellation, and propagating
   all-empty/all-nonempty status through the connected face dual.

   RL(T7,D) limit, uniform error, or forward/reverse limiting transfer. Ordinary
   mean-one SignedBase hypotheses alone do not supply these analytic identities.

   Actual dilution, reverse orientation, eventual prime choice, symmetric deficit
   and bounded scaling still need to be connected to the available interfaces.

   remain to be proved. The baseline finite_host_conditional has not been
