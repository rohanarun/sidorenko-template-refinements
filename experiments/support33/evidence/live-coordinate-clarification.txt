Additive clarification after the original static reviews

The live tuple has six coordinates not only immediately after opening a
new vertex, but also after the first vector of some pairs has completed
and the usual forgetting step has run. In zero-based vector positions:

12, pair(2,9): live[2,4,3,1,0,9]
38, pair(1,12): live[1,9,11,7,6,12]
44, pair(8,9): live[9,11,7,6,12,8]

The new coordinate starts at zero but can rise on that first vector's
defect transition. Therefore merely qualifying the original 'at most
five points at any time' as 'five retained coordinates' is insufficient.
Six coordinates must be permitted in both implementation sizing and
accurate prose. The proposed proof-text correction does so and does not
rely on a five-coordinate bound.

This observation was checked by an independently authored traversal of
the pinned pair-order metadata, not by another DP execution. It changes
neither the recurrence nor its finite results, completion lemma, tail
argument or coefficient33. The historical root review remains unchanged
and this note records the later clarification explicitly.
